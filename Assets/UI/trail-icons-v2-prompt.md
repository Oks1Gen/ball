# Промпт иконок тематических трейлов (v2)

Единый промпт для внешней генерации (ChatGPT) десяти отдельных иконок трейлов, 31.10.2026. Стиль повторяет `prompts.json` (толстая чёрная обводка, плоская мультяшная заливка, мяч справа сверху, хвост влево вниз). Готовые PNG сохранять как `<имя>-trail.png`, затем дописать их в `prompts.json` и загрузить как Roblox Image только с разрешения пользователя.

```text
Create 10 SEPARATE images: one icon per trail, in the order below. Each image is its own standalone square PNG (1024x1024) on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Never put several icons into one image: no sprite sheet, no grid, no collage. If you can only make one image per reply, make #1 now and continue with the next number every time I write "next".

SHARED STYLE (identical on all 10 so they read as one set):
Roblox simulator shop icon. One classic black-and-white soccer ball in the upper right with a thick themed comet swoosh trailing toward the lower left. Same composition, same ball size and same angle on every icon; only the swoosh changes. The whole silhouette sits fully inside the canvas with 12% empty transparent padding on every side. Bright, vibrant, high-contrast cartoon with simple rounded readable shapes. EXTRA THICK solid pure black (#000000) outer outline and bold black inner lines. Flat, highly saturated candy colors with just two clean hard-edged cel-shading tones and only a minimal tiny highlight. Matte illustrative finish: no soft gradients, no glossy shine, no bloom, no glow, no light rays, no cast shadow, no photorealism. Any small extra shapes (drops, stars, rocks, feathers, sparks) are solid flat shapes with the same thick black outline. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no frame, no tile, no background.

THE 10 ICONS:
1. Gale Trail: the swoosh is made of curling white and pale sky-blue wind ribbons (#F4FBFF, #A0E0FF) ending in 2-3 spiral wind curls.
2. Tidal Trail: the swoosh is a rolling ocean wave in deep blue and bright blue (#1E82FF, #28A0FF) with a white foam crest curling over it and 3 round water droplets.
3. Volcanic Trail: the swoosh is fiery lava in hot red, orange and yellow (#FF3054, #FF8228, #FFD23C) with flame tongues and a few dark basalt rock chunks with orange cracks.
4. Electric Trail: the swoosh is a jagged zigzag lightning bolt in bright yellow (#FFD628) with a pale cream core and a few small light-blue (#82DEFF) zigzag sparks.
5. Frost Trail: the swoosh is built from sharp ice crystal shards in ice cyan and royal blue (#96E6FF, #3CA0FF) with one small snowflake.
6. Earthquake Trail: the swoosh is cracked brown stone (#C98A3E, #5A3E28) with jagged orange magma cracks and 3 flying rock chunks.
7. Void Trail: the swoosh is a swirling dark vortex in deep purple and violet (#2A0A4A, #B54BFF) with a hot-pink edge (#FF5FD8), spiraling into a small black hole at its tail.
8. Cosmic Trail: the swoosh is a strip of night sky in deep indigo (#30208C) with magenta and teal edges (#FF5FD8, #6FF3FF), holding small flat white four-point stars and one tiny ringed planet.
9. Rainbow Trail: the swoosh is one thick band of distinct hard-edged stripes (red, yellow, green, cyan, violet) with one small yellow cartoon star.
10. Divine Trail: the swoosh is a golden sweep (#FFCD3C, #FFA22B) shaped like a fan of white angel feathers, with a small golden halo floating above the ball.
```
