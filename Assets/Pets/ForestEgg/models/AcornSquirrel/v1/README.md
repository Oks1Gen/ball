# Acorn Squirrel v1

Static Forest Egg model made in Blender 5.2.3 in background mode. The second pet in the [approved Forest Egg sheet](../../../concepts-v4/forest-egg-five-pets-v4.png) supplies the caramel body, cream muzzle and ear insets, amber eyes, short paws, and cocoa cap. The reference shows one angle. The rear was modeled as a plain caramel cube with the acorn tail attached at the back.

Editable files and exports are in `model/`. The body is two Blender units per side with a ground pivot and front facing −Y. The tail shell and cap are separate meshes. Three packed textures carry the body markings, amber irises, and curl accent. The portable files embed the same textures.

The evaluated model has 9,678 triangles, with 2,728 on the largest mesh. The BLEND, GLB, and FBX were reimported from isolated copies and rendered under the same light. Bounds and orientation are recorded in `work/verification.json`. The two review images are `work/front-three-quarter.png` and `work/rear-three-quarter.png`. The source is `work/build.py`.
