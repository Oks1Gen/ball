# Frost Egg

The five pets follow the soft square proportions in the [supplied reference](reference.png). Their colors follow the later request for brighter Roblox simulator art. Rear geometry continues the shapes visible in the reference. The portrait camera mirrors the asymmetric tails of Snow Pup, Aurora Lynx and Glacier Dragon in its staging copy.
