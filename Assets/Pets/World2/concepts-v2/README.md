## Palette direction

- Kelp: vivid lime, coral, emerald, turquoise and warm gold.
- Leviathan: strong royal blue, electric violet-blue, cyan, navy and deep teal.
- Treasure: copper orange, vivid coral/red, raspberry, turquoise, gold and ruby.
- Abyss: indigo, rich magenta, saturated violet and electric cyan.
- Jelly: intensely tinted translucent aqua, rose, magenta, royal blue and purple.

Softness comes from geometry/lighting, not washed-out color. Small ivory face patches remain for contrast. All creatures keep one square combined head/body, flat cube planes, tiny feet and a few broad species features. Marine feet may be fins or tentacles. No foliage/costumes or fine repeated detail.
